V-SLICE TOOLS

Everything for putting a mod together in the format the game reads now. Each one runs on its own, here in the browser — nothing you open is uploaded anywhere.

🎵

Chart Converter

Old charts, and whole Psych or Codename mods, into V-Slice charts and a mod folder.

chart .json, a mod folder 🕺

Character Editor

Animations, offsets and the health icon, with the character dancing as you set them.

.png, atlas .xml, character .json 🖼️

Sheet Packer

A folder of frames packed into one spritesheet with the Sparrow XML beside it.

a PNG sequence, or a .zip 🏙️

Level Editor

Story weeks: the songs in them, the art behind them and the props on top.

.png, .xml, level .json, .zip 🎬

Cutscene Editor

Stage a cutscene and write out the script that plays it.

.fnfs, stage .json, .zip of art

ON A PHONE

Add this to your home screen and it opens like an app, keeps working with no signal, and remembers what you were doing.

Hand the tools a .zip rather than a folder — phones have no folder picker — and what comes out is a zip too, ready to unpack into mods/.

Made for Friday Night Funkin'. Runs entirely on your own device.
